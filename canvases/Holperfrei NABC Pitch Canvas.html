NABC Pitch Canvas

1Hookabout 9 s

This summer Dresden sent a special vehicle through every street for 3 months. It cost about €380,000, and the result is a snapshot. The checks before were in 2015 and 2021.

2Needabout 36 s

The road planners at the city look after about 1,490 km of road with little staff and money.

For 5 to 6 years at a time, they don't see new potholes until a citizen complains. The longer damage waits, the more the repair costs.

They need a current picture of the roads, every week.

3Approachabout 90 s

A camera kit on garbage trucks, which already drive every street.

AI on the device spots potholes and blurs faces and number plates.

The planner gets a map with photos and a severity rank, sends one crew to a cluster of repairs, and the next weekly pass shows if the repair held.

Start: 3 months, one district, a few trucks.

VisionEvery road in Dresden checked every week
4Benefitsabout 18 s

A road picture every week instead of every 5 to 6 years.

The city breaks even if it saves €104k per year through earlier, cheaper repairs. Safer roads on top.

5Competitionabout 18 s

vialytics (Stuttgart) does the same with smartphones in 500+ cities. Dresden also has its own survey vehicle and free software.

Our edge, still to prove: faces and plates blurred on the device, severity ranking, a local team. Risks: detection accuracy and truck coverage.

61 sentence rounding offabout 9 s

Three months, one district, a few trucks: let Dresden see its roads every week, before the next complaint, not after.
