Need:
Context, Problem, Need, Gap, Driver

Problem: Dresden only checks its roads every 5 to 6 years, and one check costs about €380,000.

Gap: In between, nobody knows about new potholes until someone complains.

Who: The road planners at the city. They don't have much staff or money.

Driver: The longer damage waits, the more the repair costs.

Model
Approach:
Proposed approach

Idea: Put cameras on garbage trucks. They drive down every street anyway.

AI: spots potholes in the pictures and blurs faces and number plates.

Planner: gets a map with photos of the damage and picks what to fix first.

Start: try it for 3 months in one district with a few trucks.

Considerations/Competition/Alternatives/Risks
List considerations, alternatives, competition, risks

Competition

  • vialytics: does the same thing, used by 500+ cities
  • RoadBotics (USA)

Alternatives

  • The city's survey vehicle every 5 to 6 years
  • Free software the city is building
  • Inspection drives and citizen reports

Why us (still to prove)

  • Faces and plates blurred on the device
  • Ranks damage by how bad it is
  • Local team in Dresden

Risks

  • The AI might miss damage or give false alarms
  • Trucks may not drive every road
  • Privacy rules and slow city buying
