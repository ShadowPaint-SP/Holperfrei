Triple Layer BMC : Holperfrei

Layer 1 of 3 · Economic layer (classic Business Model Canvas)

Key partners

Who are your key partners?

  • Municipal waste operator (garbage trucks as camera carriers)
  • DVB (public transport buses)
  • Delivery services (DHL etc.)
  • City of Dresden, Saxon road authority
  • TU Dresden, research institutes
  • Hardware and cloud suppliers

Key activities

  • Collecting and labeling road images
  • Training and improving the AI model
  • Building dashboard and API
  • Pilots with city and fleets
  • GDPR compliance (blurring)

Key resources

  • AI model and Dresden road dataset
  • Camera units on partner vehicles
  • Cloud platform

Value proposition

What value do you deliver?

  • Find damage early, so repairs cost less
  • Every street checked every week
  • No extra staff or vehicles
  • Damage map with severity ranking
  • Ready reports for the council

Customer relationships

  • Dedicated support for city departments
  • Self-service dashboard with alerts
  • Pilot projects and co-creation

Channels

  • Direct sales and public tenders
  • Pilot in Dresden as reference
  • Web dashboard and API
  • Smart City events

Customer segments

Who are your customers?

  • City road departments (Dresden, then Saxony)
  • Construction companies (repair jobs)
  • Insurers
  • Navigation and map providers
  • Fleet operators

Cost structure

  • Fixed: team salaries
  • Fixed: cloud hosting and storage
  • Fixed: AI training and development
  • Fixed: legal and GDPR advice
  • Variable: camera hardware per vehicle
  • Variable: installation and maintenance
  • Variable: fee per truck to fleet partners
  • Variable: data labeling

Revenue streams

  • Yearly city subscription per km of road
  • One-time setup and pilot fees
  • Data licensing to insurers
  • Data licensing to navigation providers
  • Repair job lists for construction companies
  • Grants in early phase (EXIST, mFUND)

Triple Layer BMC : Holperfrei

Layer 2 of 3 · Environmental layer (life-cycle view)

Supplies & outsourcing

  • Camera and edge-computing hardware from suppliers
  • Cloud provider, ideally on green energy
  • Installation by own team, no extra vehicles

Production

  • Assembling camera units
  • Training the AI model (energy use)
  • Software development

Materials

  • Cameras, chips, mounts, cables
  • Durable, repairable parts

Functional value

  • Weekly condition check of a city’s road network
  • Detect and rank road damage, without dedicated inspection trips

End of life

  • Move cameras to new vehicles
  • Take back and refurbish units
  • Certified e-waste recycling

Distribution

  • Mounted on vehicles that drive anyway
  • Software delivered online

Use phase

  • Low power, runs on vehicle power
  • Images processed on the device
  • Only results uploaded, less data traffic
  • No extra driving needed

Environmental impacts

  • Electronic waste from hardware
  • Raw materials in chips and cameras
  • Energy for AI training
  • Energy for cloud storage
  • Small extra weight and power on vehicles

Environmental benefits

  • No extra inspection drives, less CO₂
  • Early small repairs need less asphalt
  • Fewer big road rebuilds
  • Smoother roads, less fuel and tyre wear
  • Longer road life
  • Better repair planning, fewer crew trips

Layer 3 of 3 · Social layer (stakeholder view)

Local communities

  • Dresden residents and road users
  • TU Dresden and student community
  • Local construction companies
  • Waste operator and its drivers

Governance

  • Student startup, founders own it
  • Clear data protection rules
  • Open about data use with the city

Employees

  • Small tech team in Dresden
  • Fair Werkstudent and local jobs

Social value

  • Safer roads for drivers, cyclists and pedestrians
  • Public money spent where it is needed most

Societal culture

  • Trust in a city that fixes problems fast
  • Responsible, privacy-first use of data

Scale of outreach

  • Dresden, then Saxony, then Germany
  • Small towns that could never afford surveys

End user

  • Road maintenance planners at the city
  • Indirectly every citizen who uses the roads
  • Need: find damage early, decide what to fix first

Social impacts

  • Privacy concerns about cameras
  • Drivers may feel watched
  • Fear that inspection jobs change
  • Trust risk if AI makes mistakes

Social benefits

  • Fewer accidents and injuries
  • Fewer damaged cars and bikes
  • Faster answers to citizen complaints
  • Tax money saved
  • Inspectors freed for higher-value work
  • Fairer repairs across all districts
