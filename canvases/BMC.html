Business Model Canvas: AI Potholes

Camera kits for municipal vehicles, with licensed AI street monitoring

Key partners

Who helps us deliver?

  • Camera, sensor and computing hardware suppliers
  • Municipal fleet departments for installation and operation
  • Connectivity and hosting providers

Cities provide the vehicles. We supply the system and software.

Key activities

What do we do?

  • Develop and test camera kits
  • Train and validate detection AI
  • Build dashboards and reports
  • Detect Potholes and other flaws in the streets

Key resources

What do we need?

  • Cameras and location sensors
  • AI model and labelled images
  • Dashboard and data processing
  • Engineering team and pilot access

Value proposition

What value do we deliver?

  • Scan roads during existing municipal vehicle routes
  • Detect potholes and other visible surface defects
  • Report geolocated findings with images and severity estimates
  • Track changes across repeat scans
  • efficient and early detection to prevent future costs

Authorities review findings and decide what action to take.

Customer relationships

How do we support customers?

  • Pilot projects with road departments
  • Setup guidance and training
  • Dedicated technical support
  • Updates and annual renewals

Channels

How do we reach customers?

  • Direct municipal sales
  • Local pilots and demonstrations
  • Municipal networks and events
  • Cold aquisition
  • Trade fairs

Customer segments

Who pays for the system?

  • Cities and municipalities
  • Municipal road and infrastructure departments
  • Other public road authorities as a later expansion

Deployment on existing garbage trucks, road sweepers and other suitable municipal vehicles.

The public authority is the customer; municipal fleets carry the equipment.

Cost structure

What are our main costs?

Fixed costs

Hardware and AI development, field validation, software maintenance.

Variable costs

Camera kits, delivery, warranty, connectivity, processing, storage and customer support.

Revenue streams

How do we make money?

One-time product sale

Camera and sensor kit for each equipped vehicle.

Annual software licence

Option A: per equipped vehicle.
Option B: one general licence per authority.

Licence includes AI detection, dashboard, reports and updates. Pricing model to be decided.
