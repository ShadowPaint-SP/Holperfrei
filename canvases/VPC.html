Value Proposition Canvas: AI Potholes

Customer: cities, municipalities and their road departments

Products & services

  • Vehicle-mounted cameras
  • AI detection licence
  • Map and image reports
  • Updates and support

Gain creators

  • Scan during existing fleet routes
  • Refresh maps through repeat scans
  • Track changes over time
  • Support maintenance planning

Pain relievers

  • Automate initial defect screening
  • Locate findings with image evidence
  • Limit images, blur faces and plates
  • Pilot before wider deployment

Customer jobs

  • Monitor road condition
  • Prioritise inspections
  • Plan maintenance
  • Document decisions

Gains

  • Up-to-date road information
  • Less inspection effort
  • Better budget allocation
  • Safer road infrastructure

Pains

  • Limited staff and budgets
  • Manual, infrequent inspections
  • Defects discovered too late
  • Privacy and investment concerns

Expected benefits to validate in a pilot: detection accuracy, route coverage, inspection effort and potential cost savings.
