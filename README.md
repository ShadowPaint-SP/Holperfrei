# Holperfrei

Holperfrei is an idea for helping city road departments find road damage earlier. Cameras mounted on garbage trucks would capture streets during their regular routes. AI would identify cracks and potholes, rank their severity, and produce a weekly damage map for the city without extra inspection vehicles or staff.

The proposed business model is an annual subscription priced per kilometre of road, starting with a pilot in Dresden.
