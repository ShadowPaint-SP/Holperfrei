# Value Propositions: Holperfrei

*Idea-pitch version, November 2026. One value proposition per customer group. The city is the paying customer; the others are partners, later data buyers, or the people who benefit. 

## The product in one sentence

Small AI cameras on garbage trucks that already drive every street find potholes and road damage, and the city gets a weekly map ranked by urgency.

## Overview

| Customer group | Role | Their main pain | Our promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| City road department | **Paying customer** | Damage found too late, expensive surveys, liability risk | Every street, every week, 25% cheaper than the market leader |
| Garbage truck company | **Partner** (carries our camera) | Disputes after accidents, overflowing bins, illegal dumping | Free dashcam + bin alerts + €300 per truck per year, zero effort |
| Smaller towns and Landkreise | **Paying customer** (expansion) | Few staff, rural roads checked rarely | Same weekly map without hiring inspectors |
| Insurers, map apps, construction firms | **Data buyers** (from year 3) | Missing, outdated road-quality data | Up-to-date road-damage data for every street |
| Citizens and cyclists | **Users** (don't pay) | Potholes, bike crashes, car damage | Roads fixed faster, safer bike lanes |

## 1. City road department (paying customer)

**Who:** the Straßen- und Tiefbauamt Dresden and similar departments in other cities. The decision-makers are the department head, the road maintenance planner, and the council that approves the budget.

**Value proposition**
> For city road departments that must keep hundreds of kilometres of road safe with little staff and money, Holperfrei checks every street every week using garbage trucks that already drive there. They find damage while it is still cheap to fix and can prove they inspected. Unlike one-off survey vehicles or smartphone apps that city staff must drive around, it needs no extra staff or vehicles and costs 25% less than the market leader.

**Customer jobs**
- Monitor the condition of the whole road network.
- Decide which repairs come first with a limited budget.
- Plan maintenance and justify the budget to the council.
- Document inspections under the *Verkehrssicherungspflicht* (legal duty to keep roads safe).

**Pains → pain relievers**

| Pain | How we relieve it |
|---|---|
| Inspections are manual and rare; a survey is a snapshot every few years | Automatic weekly scan of every street |
| Damage is found too late, so a small crack becomes an expensive rebuild | Early detection while repairs are cheap |
| Surveys are expensive (Dresden 2026: €380k for one snapshot) | €75/km/yr; €108k/yr for Dresden, 28% of that one survey |
| Liability claims when someone is hurt by an unrecorded pothole | Weekly, timestamped proof of inspection |
| Too few staff to drive around looking | No extra staff or vehicles needed |
| Privacy concerns about cameras in public | AI on the device, only damage images uploaded, faces and plates blurred |

**Gains → gain creators**

| Gain they want | How we create it |
|---|---|
| Up-to-date picture of all roads | Live map, refreshed weekly |
| Better use of the repair budget | Damage ranked by severity; repair list ready for crews |
| Fewer complaints from citizens | Faster fixes |
| Strong arguments for the council | Ready-made reports and before/after evidence |
| Being seen as a smart, modern city | A visible Smart City success story |

**Numbers that convince them**
- Cost: 1,440 km × €75 = **€108k/yr**.
- Saving (conservative): €11.2m maintenance budget × 10% saving from early repair × 50% credited to us = **€560k/yr**.
- **Return ≈ 5×**: every €1 saves about €5.

## 2. Garbage truck company (partner)

**Who:** Stadtreinigung Dresden (city-owned) and, later, waste operators in other cities. They carry our camera; they don't pay us, we pay them.

**Value proposition**
> For waste companies whose trucks drive every street anyway, Holperfrei turns each truck into a smart vehicle. They get a free dashcam for accidents, alerts for overflowing bins and illegal dumping, and €300 per truck per year, and they do nothing extra. We install and maintain everything.

**Pains → pain relievers**

| Pain | How we relieve it |
|---|---|
| Disputes after accidents, no footage | Free dashcam footage |
| Overflowing bins and illegal dumping found late | Automatic alerts from the same camera |
| Trucks worn out by bad roads | Bad roads get reported and fixed faster |
| No time or staff for new projects | Zero effort: we install, maintain and handle data protection |

**Gains → gain creators**

| Gain | How we create it |
|---|---|
| Extra income | €300 per truck per year |
| Good public image | "Our trucks help make Dresden's roads safer" |
| Better route planning | Road-condition data for their own routes |

## 3. Smaller towns and Landkreise (expansion customers)

**Who:** mid-sized towns (e.g. Radebeul, Pirna, Freital) and Landkreise responsible for Kreisstraßen.

**Value proposition**
> For towns and districts with long road networks but only one or two engineers, Holperfrei provides the same weekly road map as a big city, without hiring inspectors or paying for a survey vehicle, starting from €50/km/yr.

**Why it fits:** few staff, rural roads checked rarely, and faster decisions (the mayor and one engineer decide). They can start with the cheaper Essentials tier.

---

## 4. Insurers, map apps and construction firms (data buyers, from year 3)

**Value proposition**
> For companies that need to know the real condition of roads, Holperfrei sells up-to-date road-damage data for every street, collected weekly, which nobody else has at this frequency.

| Buyer | Pain | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Insurers (car, municipal liability) | Pay out for pothole damage | Risk data to reduce claims |
| Navigation and map providers | No road-quality data for routing | Bad-road warnings and smoother routes |
| Construction companies | Need proof of condition before and after a job | Before/after records and lists of repair jobs |

---

## 5. Citizens and cyclists (users who benefit)

**Value proposition**
> For people who walk, cycle and drive in Dresden, Holperfrei means potholes get found and fixed faster: fewer bike crashes, less car damage, safer streets.

They don't pay, but they are the reason the city cares and a strong emotional hook for the pitch.

## What we still need to validate in the pilot

- How much of the network the trucks really cover each week.
- How much inspection time the city saves.
